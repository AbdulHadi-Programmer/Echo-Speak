The Importance of Financial Literacy for Young Adults
Introduction
Financial literacy is a crucial life skill that young adults need to secure their future. Understanding money management helps in making informed financial decisions.

Key Aspects of Financial Literacy
1. Budgeting - Tracking income and expenses prevents overspending.
2. Saving & Investing - Early investments compound over time, ensuring financial growth.
3. Debt Management - Avoiding unnecessary debt and managing loans wisely.
4. Understanding Credit - Maintaining a good credit score is essential for future financial needs.
5. Retirement Planning - Starting early helps in securing financial stability later in life.

Common Financial Mistakes
* Overspending on non-essential items.
* Ignoring emergency funds.
* Not investing early.
* Depending on credit cards without understanding interest rates.

Conclusion
Financial literacy empowers young adults to make smart money decisions, ensuring financial stability and independence in the long run.
